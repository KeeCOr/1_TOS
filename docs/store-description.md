# Swordmasters Ascent — Steam Store Description

## Short Description

A turn-based roguelite where every swordmaster in the tower wants you dead. Build your mastery, climb higher, and survive one floor at a time.

---

## Full Description

**Swordmasters Ascent** is a turn-based roguelite set in a tower where every inhabitant — and every enemy — is a swordmaster. You are no different. Arm yourself with technique, manage your stamina under pressure, and push deeper into an endless tower where each floor brings tougher opponents and harder decisions.

Combat is built around a positional grid system. Distance between you and your opponent determines which attacks land, which skills reach, and how much stamina each action costs. Close the gap to land heavy strikes, fall back to buy recovery time, or hold your ground and counter. Every choice costs stamina — the only way to recover it is to move, so standing still is never free.

Between floors, choose your rewards carefully. Equipment changes your stat ceiling. Items extend your options in tight spots. Titles reshape how your character grows. No two runs play the same, and no two swordmasters fight the same way. Your build evolves floor by floor, and so does the pressure bearing down on you.

With no victory condition and infinite floors to climb, Swordmasters Ascent is a game about legacy — how far did you get, and what will you do differently next time?

---

## Key Features

- **Positional Combat System** — Distance and row placement determine which attacks hit. Manage a five-position grid to outmaneuver opponents who fight by the same rules you do.
- **Stamina Economy** — Every action draws from a shared stamina pool. Movement is the only way to recover. Master the rhythm of spending and recovering to survive longer.
- **Deep Roguelite Progression** — Unlock titles, gear, and abilities across runs. Customize your approach each time with equipment, magic, and throwable items that complement your playstyle.
- **Infinite Tower Climbing** — No finish line. Your high-floor record is the only score that matters. Each run teaches you something the last one didn't.
- **Fast, Readable Sessions** — A full run can end in minutes or stretch into a long climb. The loop is tight: name your swordmaster, roll your stats, fight, collect rewards, repeat.
